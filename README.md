## What is GhostKernel?

GhostKernel is a full YouTube Music client for KornDog Records, built for the way Joey actually listens: background playback, real audio quality, and a KornDog identity from top to bottom.

It plays music directly. There's no separate "brain" service and no external playback bridge — GhostKernel resolves streams, decodes audio, and plays it, all in one app.

---

## Features

* **Real Audio-Only Playback** — Resolves genuine audio-only streams (Opus/AAC) instead of settling for muxed video fallbacks.
* **Loudness Boost** — Real PCM gain via a custom audio processor, not just volume scaling.
* **Music DNA** — Genre/decade/mood preferences drive Quick Picks and mood-based EQ presets.
* **Ghost Picks** — Personalized home recommendations (visual layer live; real listening-based personalization in active development, see Roadmap).
* **YouTube Library Sync** — Real liked songs/playlists from your YouTube Music account.
* **Background Playback** — Screen off, music on. Full Android media notification controls.
* **Smart Cache** — Tracks pre-cache in the background for instant playback.
* **Queue Management** — Build, save, and navigate queues. Persistent across restarts.
* **Android Auto** — Driving mode support.
* **Lyrics** — Fetch and display lyrics.
* **KornDog Theme** — Deep purple + lime green branding throughout, Zombie Kitty mascot, Vinyl Therapy identity.

## Roadmap

**Ghost Brain Memory v1** *(in progress)* — GhostKernel is beginning to learn from real listening behavior (meaningful listens, skips, likes) to make Ghost Picks smarter over time. Local-first, no cloud, no AI claims yet.

## Built On

GhostKernel started as a fork of SoundPod by Arun Nechully, which is itself built on the shoulders of NewPipe Extractor, ViMusic, InnerTune, music-you, and RiMusic. Full credits to all original authors. GhostKernel has since diverged significantly with its own audio resolution pipeline, KornDog identity, Music DNA system, and Ghost Brain listening memory.
